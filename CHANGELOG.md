# Changelog

What changed in each version of Toolbox. Each GitHub release uses its version's section as its notes, and
Toolbox's Software Update window shows them before you install. Add changes under **Unreleased** as they
land; `scripts/publish.sh <version>` turns that section into the release.

## Unreleased

## 0.3.1 — 2026-10-20

### Changed
- The search fields in the launcher and on the home screen show Toolbox's mark, the magnifying glass with code brackets, instead of a plain magnifying glass.

### Fixed
- The quick launcher no longer has a thin dark outline around its corners.

### Privacy
- Toolbox now sends basic usage data, like when it's opened and which version you're on, so we can see how it's used. It isn't linked to your name or email, and your files never leave your Mac.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-20

### New look
- A new app icon: a magnifying glass around code brackets. The menu bar icon, the install disk and the pages Toolbox shares with your phone wear it too.
- Buttons, switches and highlights now follow the icon: black in Light Mode and white in Dark Mode. Selections on PDFs, scans and photos are blue, so they stand out on the page.

### Fixed
- After an update, Finder and the Dock show the new version's icon right away instead of the old one.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-20

### New
- **Teleprompter.** Read your script right under the camera notch, or in a floating window you can put anywhere. It scrolls as you speak and waits when you stop, pauses while you hover over it, and stays hidden when you share or record your screen.
- Split a script into cues with `#` headings, jump to any of them and go back to where you were. Notes in [square brackets] are dimmed and never counted.
- Use your phone as a teleprompter remote over Wi-Fi: turn a dial to scroll, change speed and jump between cues.
- Press ⌃⌥⌘T in any app to show or hide the teleprompter.
- **Software updates.** Toolbox checks for new versions, shows what changed and installs them for you. Look for the banner on the home screen, or choose Check for Updates in the launcher, the menu bar or the Toolbox menu.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-20

- First release: 83 tools for video, audio, images, PDFs, documents, archives, on-device AI and small-business billing, with the quick launcher, drag wheels and onboarding.
